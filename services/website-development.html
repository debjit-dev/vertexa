Stack flexibility

Static, CMS-driven, or a custom web app — your call

Not every site needs the same foundation. A five-page brochure site doesn't need the same setup as a growing content site. We'll recommend the simplest stack that meets your goals: a static site if that's all you need, a lightweight CMS if your team wants to publish without a developer, or a fuller custom build if the site needs to do more than show pages.
