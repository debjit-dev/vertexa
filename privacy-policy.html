Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the details you provide — name, email, and project information — to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & analytics

We use basic, privacy-preserving performance analytics to understand how visitors navigate the site so we can continuously optimize speed and accessibility. We do not run third-party advertising trackers or invasive fingerprinting scripts.

You can adjust your cookie choices at any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in our site footer or by modifying your browser settings.

Data retention & security

Inquiry and quote submissions are stored in encrypted form and retained only for as long as necessary to service your project discussion. You may request deletion of your contact data at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data rights can be sent directly to hello@vertexa.agency.
