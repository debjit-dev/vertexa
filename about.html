Our journey

How we got here

Built deliberately from day one to close the gap between mockup design and production performance.

2021 • Founding

Started with zero handoffs

Vertexa was founded in Salt Lake Sector V, Kolkata, by owners Debjit Das & Upasana Roy — software engineers and designers tired of watching client projects break between design files, code, and hosting servers.

2022 • Quality Standard

100% Responsive-first protocol

Mandated that every layout must be designed and verified on physical phones, tablets, and desktops before delivery — eliminating the dreaded "looks good only on desktop" trap.

2024 • Global Infrastructure

Managed edge CDN & hosting launch

Introduced our integrated hosting service with global CDN routing, auto-renewing SSL certificates, and 24/7 synthetic uptime monitoring so clients never worry about server maintenance.

2026 • Today & Beyond

50+ High-performing sites shipped

Serving businesses across 10+ industries with proven SEO visibility gains, sub-second load times, and rock-solid 99.9% uptime.
